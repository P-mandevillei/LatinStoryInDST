This project turns a Latin playscript into a mod in Don't Starve Together (DST).

## Architecture

- `modmain.lua` registers assets and prefabs, then attaches `libellusprogress` to each player on the server. `modinfo.lua` contains mod metadata.
- `scripts/libellus.lua` holds dialogue by chapter, including speakers, facing targets, and scene boundaries. Chapters one through six have gameplay triggers; chapter-five line 17 remains a repeatable flute-use event.
- `scripts/helpers.lua` centralizes timing, distances, health settings, spawn-location searches, and inspection-string registration.
- `scripts/components/libellusprogress.lua` owns each player's saved story position, dialogue scheduling, speaker selection, and proximity/entry triggers. It finds or spawns the player's Publius and casa. Recruited Publius saves with the player and travels between shards.
- `scripts/prefabs/` constructs entities. `publius.lua` provides the follower using Wilson's appearance; `casa.lua` builds the house, courtyard, Lupa enclosure, and 3 to 5 soldiers, manages territorial aggression, and restores encounter references after loading. `miles.lua` supplies armed soldiers using WX-78's appearance with 1,000 HP; `lupa.lua` supplies the stationary NPC using fire hound animations.
- The `casa_supplies` mod option (default off) supplies each casa once with a native chest containing a hammer, logsuit, two Bootleg Getaways, 40 cut grass, four ice creams, a Walking Cane, ten Glommer's Goop, and `libellus_worldmap`. The saved issuance flag prevents refilling or replacing the chest; enabling the option also supplies existing casas on loading. The map prefab reuses the native map scroll and Teach action, revealing the current shard only to its reader before being consumed.
- `agricola_farm.lua` owns a nine-unit-square farm, its tilled plots, decorative crops, and Agricola. Placement searches connected savanna regions from the player's saved spawn origin. `agricola.lua` uses Woodie's appearance and a straw hat; its brain and stategraph move between crops and animate tending/harvesting without generating inventory items. Chapter-two lines 7–13 lead to chapter three and permanently clear the owned casa's Lupa and soldiers after the final speech finishes.
- `navis.lua` owns saved references to a native wooden boat, steering wheel, lamp-upgraded mast, anchor, and Senex. It spawns in nearby clear ocean and starts anchored; native equipment remains operable and retains player changes on loading. `senex.lua` uses Wolfgang's appearance, a spear, 1,000 HP, and 25 damage. His brain stays aboard the moving boat; his stategraph rechecks boarding before damage. Chapter-three line 1 requires sight and proximity, and lines 2–8 require closer proximity.
- `passer_grove.lua` places Passer's occupied birdcage in nearby forest and owns an isolated 27-unit-square grove of 81 burnt trees behind it, with a 16-unit cleared buffer. Placement protects structures, characters, and other encounters; loading restores references without clearing again. `passer.lua` reuses occupied-cage and robin animations. `passer_burnt_tree.lua` retains native burnt-tree behavior while preventing decay, pinecone drops, and regrowth. Chapter-three lines 9–10 start 15 seconds after line 8 ends (remaining delay saves); ambient line 11 repeats on sight, and nearby lines 12–14 advance to chapter four after the final speech.
- Four hammer hits destroy Passer's cage and replace it with `passer_free.lua`. `passerbrain.lua` flies the bird to the near edge of the grove; `SGpasser.lua` animates takeoff, flight, and landing before chapter-four lines 1–8 can play. Release belongs to the grove's story player even when another player hammers, and the released bird and its flight destination save with the encounter.
- After chapter-four line 8 finishes, Passer flies toward the existing Pig King at the player's effective walking speed. `rexanimalium.lua` extends native Pig Kings without replacing trading, sleep, or minigame behavior; `rex_voice.lua` supplies a replicated child talker. Nearby lines 9–13 lead to chapter five and toss a pan flute and Old Bell to the player; separate saved reward flags prevent duplicate gifts.
- `modinfo.lua` requires Bigfoot Returns (Workshop `2522543318`). Rex gives its `bell` prefab, which supplies Old Bell art, the play action, and an animated Biiigfoot encounter through `TheWorld.components.bigfooter:SummonFoot(position)`. Damage, uses, collateral damage, and warning steps follow the dependency's configuration (its default damage is 2,500). The dependency also adds its normal recipes, Glommer-statue behavior, and Littlefoot pet; enable it on the server and clients.
- `storyitems.lua` names Rex's native pan flute and bell `Saxum Somni` and `Saxum Sacrum` through the saved, replicated `named` component. It preserves the native pan flute callback and routes successful plays to chapter-five line 17 independently of progress. Chapter-five lines 1–11 follow chapter four immediately, and nearby Senex triggers lines 12–16. Senex prepares saved story sleep once in chapter five, waking within five units; his native `sleeper` component accepts flute sleep and stops combat and the brain. `SGsenex.lua` uses the player `dozy`, `sleep_loop`, and `wakeup` animations. Proximity never interrupts flute-induced deep sleep.
- `components/senexshorepush.lua` adds native `pushable` only while Senex sleeps, using DST's existing Push action and player push animations while Senex retains `sleep_loop`. One secondary click starts a shove away from the player, with ten units of travel (at least the boat diameter plus two). The shove ignores all objects aboard the starting boat (including parented mast/sail attachments) and nonblocking collision helpers, and can cross water gaps up to four units to clear land. Without a reachable landing it stops at the last point where the whole body fits on deck; a narrow island also stops the shove before it would overshoot into water. Waking interrupts movement, and saves during a shove restore the safe starting position. A saved shore home prevents the brain from returning him aboard or attacking the boat's occupants after he has been pushed ashore.
- `scripts/brains/` controls following, chasing, and returning to guard positions. `scripts/stategraphs/` controls movement and combat animations; `SGmiles.lua` rechecks the casa boundary before applying attack damage.
- `king_castle.lua` owns one shared, saved lunar-island castle, its map marker, an 18-unit-square stone boundary, and a 20-unit-square Lush Carpet floor with native Gold Flooring inside (terrain rounds to DST's four-unit grid). It clears natural clutter only on initial placement and protects structures, lunar altars, creatures, and other encounters. `rex.lua` supplies Maxwell's appearance, a Thulecite Crown, Thulecite Suit, cane, and 1,000 HP. `king_court.lua` wraps native Dragonfly, Deerclops, Bearger, and Bee Queen constructors as four 2,000-HP Animi Magni; `lupa.lua` also supplies a moving, talking 2,000-HP `king_lupa`. `kingcourtbrain.lua` keeps the peaceful court close to Rex; `kingguard` gathers the Animi close to Rex after the bell gift, waits four seconds after all survivors arrive, then switches them to their original combat brains. Gathering destinations and the target user ID save with each guard; the castle saves the remaining pause. Castle bosses suppress native offscreen/seasonal despawning, and their area attacks exclude their court allies.
- At the last line of chapter five (`DIALOG.CH5_SEP[3]`), seeing the castle advances to chapter six. Sight triggers play lines 1–2 at the castle, 3–6 at Lupa, and 7–11 at Rex. `kingscene.lua` and temporary saved `libelluscastlesleep` components hold the world clock at night, light the speaking player and Lupa, and put other characters and creatures to sleep for Lupa's conversation. `stategraphs/libellussleepstates.lua` supplies visible player sleep animations for Publius, Rex, Agricola, and soldiers. Existing sleepers and knocked-out players retain their prior state. Ending, interrupting, disconnecting, or loading restores the clock and sleep leases. Rex accepts gifts after line 11 finishes; a native `bell` gift plays line 12 immediately, invokes its instrument, sends Lupa through an open castle exit with ground-pound immunity, and gathers the Animi beside Rex for four seconds before they attack the giver. A temporary castle-only land check prevents the dependency's legacy tile-ID comparison from treating Gold Flooring as water; a hook on its actual animated Biiigfoot stomp ensures Rex dies even under low-damage configurations. Line 13 waits for his death and for Lupa to be near the player, then advances to chapter-six line 14 after finishing. Saved encounter references, pending speech delays, hostility, and an interrupted stomp resume without rebuilding the castle or consuming a second bell.
- After the human king dies, his story player's Lupa follows that player using the court brain, native hound boat hops, and a permanent follower relationship. `king_lupa` must include the native `amphibiouscreature` component, hound land/water banks, water-entry/exit callbacks, and native embarker settings: `SGhound` uses amphibious hop states even for land-to-boat jumps and calls `OnExitOcean()` unconditionally. The Python test runner loads the installed hound stategraph and amphibious component to exercise boarding, disembarking, and water landings. Line 13 also requires Lupa within `LUPA_MEETING_DISTANCE`. Recruited Lupa saves and migrates with the player instead of saving a second world copy in the castle. The defeated-king flag persists independently of the surface castle so the conversation can finish on another shard.
- Chapter-six line 14 starts near Rex animalium. Only after its speech ends can `publilius.lua` create Publilius, which reuses the native Vargling (`critter_puppy`) constructor and a replicated child talker. A dedicated native pet-leash slot preserves other pets; the slot is registered before loading saved pets. Publilius speaks line 16 once at the same time that all spawned normal pigmen and pig guards begin line 15 and their native raised-arms running animations. Rex's `libelluscelebration` component owns the 60-second timer and saved participant references. Temporary `libelluspigcelebrant` components record original locations and brains; `celebrationpigbrain.lua` runs them back and forth around Rex, and cleanup returns them to their original locations (or moving boats) and native behavior. Remaining celebration time saves. After Publilius finishes, the player speaks line 17 and invokes native `/toast` through `GetCommonEmotes().toast.data`; progress advances past the chapter after that speech. Line boundaries remain in `DIALOG.CH6_SEP`; scene timings and distances belong in `helpers.lua`.

- `scripts/dialogselector.lua` optionally adds the pause-menu dialogue selector and its RPC. `dialogselection.lua` validates choices; `screens/dialogselectorscreen.lua` supplies the UI. Current progress is mirrored to player network fields by `SetProgress`; selection changes only the sending player's chapter and line.

## Working conventions and tests

Keep story progression in the player component, encounter ownership in the casa prefab, and movement decisions in brains. Create replicated visuals and talkers before `SetPristine()`; add server-only gameplay components after the master-simulation check. Register new prefabs in `modmain.lua`.

Run the mocked Lua tests from the mod root:

```sh
lua tests/libellusprogress_test.lua
lua tests/casa_test.lua
lua tests/dialogselection_test.lua
lua tests/king_test.lua
```

These cover dialogue, spawning, persistence, client/server setup, and guard boundaries. They do not replace in-game checks of visuals, physics, and networking.
